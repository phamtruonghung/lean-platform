The Training record, from raised to filed

Who does what, in order: the Raiser drafts and sends, each Trainee answers for themselves, and HR files the printed form.

  1. 1

    Raise a Training record. Anyone holding an edit Grant that reaches the Org Unit raises it there — a supervisor, HR; an administrator may raise one anywhere. The Platform resolves existence first and scope second: an unknown Org Unit or trainer is a 404 in the Module’s own words, a write outside your Grants a 403. The record is numbered at creation from its Site’s own counter (TR-HCM-2026-00001) and is never renumbered.

  2. 2

    Fill it in as a draft, and name the Trainees. The title, the day the training was given, its length in hours, its trainer (any Employee from the Directory — a trainer who holds no Account is still nameable), and the objective. Trainees are added one at a time, so the list names real people: the same Employee twice is refused, and a departed Employee is refused the way a Work order’s assignee is. A draft is visible only to whoever could send it, so a half-typed list never reads as a fact in the register.

  3. 3

    Complete? Sending is refused until the record carries a title, a date, a duration, a trainer, an objective and at least one Trainee — refused in the Platform’s own words, naming what is missing.

  4. 4

    Send it. A deliberate act, and it asks every Trainee at once rather than in a trickle: status becomes sent, with the time and the Account that sent it, written in one statement, so two people pressing Send cannot both win and a second send is refused. Nothing notifies anybody — the Platform has no email and no push. “Sent” means the record appears in each Trainee’s own list (/training/to-confirm) and on their Home card.

  5. 5

    The Trainee answers for themselves — two doors, one write. On their own Account, or at the shared floor device by employee number and PIN, because most of a plant holds no Account; the device lists only the identified Employee’s own unanswered records, and only at Org Units it reaches. Answering for a colleague is refused on both doors — Only the Trainee can answer for themselves — with no administrator bypass. A Trainee may change their own answer, and that rewrites the same row, audited.

  6. 6

    The answer carries their Signature. Confirming may apply the Signature the Employee keeps (one image, append-only), one drawn at that moment, or none at all. Declining never carries one — the database refuses that combination. The floor device can show the kept Signature but can never set or replace it: that is the Employee’s own act on their own Account. A signature says the person was there; no answer writes employee_skills, so a confirmation is never a claim that they can do the thing.

  7. 7

    Print the form, and file it. /training/:id/form, deliberately outside the Shell so no sidebar, header or footer reaches the paper: the plant’s own bilingual form, carrying the record’s number, the trainer’s line, and each Trainee’s answer date in the Signature column — blank where they have not answered, so a hand signature can still be collected. It can be printed before everyone has confirmed, and no PDF or file is generated anywhere: the browser page is the document.

  8. 8

    Correct a sent record. After sending, anyone with an edit Grant reaching the Org Unit may correct the record’s own facts, add a Trainee who attended late, or remove one who should not be there — each audited, and removing an answered Trainee takes their answer with it. The trainer’s evaluation (paper, or job training) is settable on a sent record too, because it is the trainer’s reading and not a correction. A draft may be discarded; a sent record never — it is evidence.

Every write here is audited. Nothing in this flow grants a skill or a qualification.
